YouTube offers an official download option for your own uploaded videos when eligible.

Why doesn't SaveWise download every YouTube video?

YouTube does not generally authorize third-party tools to download or store its audiovisual content. SaveWise does not scrape streams, remove protections, or provide unauthorized copies. If you own a video, use YouTube Studio's official download workflow.
