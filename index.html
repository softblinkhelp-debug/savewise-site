GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does this site download every link directly?

No. This starter version identifies supported domains and directs you to official saving options. It does not fetch media or bypass platform restrictions.

Can I save any public video?

Public visibility does not automatically grant download or reuse rights. Follow the platform’s terms and copyright law, and get permission where required.

Is my link stored?

This version checks the link locally in your browser. It does not send it to our application server or save it in local storage. Your hosting provider may process standard web request data when you visit the site.

Can I download my own YouTube videos?

YouTube provides a download option for your own uploaded videos through YouTube Studio, subject to its current requirements.

Will this website be free?

The code is free. Hosting is planned for Cloudflare Pages’ free plan, which is subject to Cloudflare’s current plan limits and terms. A custom domain is optional and usually costs money.
